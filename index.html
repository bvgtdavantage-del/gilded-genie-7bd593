De officioThe Office

Most capital chases what is offered.
Ours waits for what is forced.

Constant Private is a multi-asset family office operating from Dubai across the UAE and the Gulf. The mandate is narrow by design: situations where the seller's timeline is shorter than the asset's value — estates in transition, lenders exiting, operators who built well and must leave early.

We work by introduction, not solicitation. Counterparties deal with principals, not committees. Diligence is done quietly, terms are kept simple, and closings happen on the dates agreed.

Alongside proprietary acquisitions, the office structures capital for a small circle of UHNW families and single-family offices seeking exposure to the same situations.
